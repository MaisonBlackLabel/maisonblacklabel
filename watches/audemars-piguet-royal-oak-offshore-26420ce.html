About This Reference

The Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Offshore Chronograph 26420CE represents the pinnacle of the Offshore lineage. Reference 26420CE.OO.A043VE.01 is distinguished by its monochromatic blue execution: a blue tapisserie ceramic dial, blue ceramic octagonal bezel with eight titanium screws, and a 43mm black and blue ceramic case.

The blue dial features the Royal Oak Offshore signature tapisserie pattern with three chronograph counters, date aperture, and luminescent baton indices. Powered by AP's in-house Caliber 4401 — self-winding, 40 jewels, 70-hour power reserve, exhibition caseback.

This 2023 example is in excellent condition with the original box and papers. Authenticated and insured, ready to ship nationwide from West Palm Beach, FL.
